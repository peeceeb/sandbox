Best answer to the user’s query (“CRR3”)
- Under the CRR (Capital Requirements Regulation) framework, CRR3 introduces an output floor that affects Risk‑Weighted Assets (RWA), not CET1 directly (CRR3_GD_Docs_Implemented.pdf, p.6). Since CRR3 go‑live in 2025, floored RWA have been determined using the FRTB standardized approach for market risk, even where unfloored market risk RWA are not based on it (p.5). Unfloored market risk RWA are based on MR‑SA components: SBM (delta/vega/curvature), DRC, and RRAO (p.5; CRR3_Output_floor_Latest.pdf, p.3).
- For unfloored RWA overall, CRR Article 92(4) governs Credit, Market, Operational, and CVA risks; Market Risk and CVA RWA are derived by multiplying own funds requirements by 12.5 per Article 92(6)(b) (CRR3_Output_floor_Latest.pdf, pp.2–3). Operational Risk uses the SMA, though not implemented in FDW; CVA and OpRisk reporting use artificial records/IFRSPOSC3 via MRORTree.xls (p.3).
- For the floor, product‑level constraints apply: derivatives/SFT must use SA‑CCR (and SVAA), IMM not allowed; credit/CCR must use CRSA, IRBA not allowed; CRSA floor includes minimum loss coverage deduction (orgexpbsa reduced by npeshrtflrep). Floored credit risk RWA are reported as RWABSA in t_rwabsa of bii‑sa‑tree (CRR3_Output_floor_Latest.pdf, p.6; CRR3_GD_Docs_Implemented.pdf, p.6).
- Additional rule: under SA‑CCR for floored RWA, internal ratings must not be used to assign supervisory factors (CRR Art. 280c(5)); while not yet implemented in Engines, its impact is de minimis (CRR3_GD_Docs_Implemented.pdf, p.6).
- Timeline: Current capital‑binding measures remain VaR/IRC/SVAR until FRTB (IMA/SA) becomes capital‑binding in January 2027 (CRR3_Output_floor_Latest.pdf, p.3).
